# Research Logging Task

Use this when the user conducts research, experiments, validation, literature review, qualitative observation, calibration, or source checking.

## Research entry requirements

A useful research entry includes:

- question
- hypothesis or expectation
- method
- inputs or sources
- result
- interpretation
- confidence
- limitations
- what changed
- next test
- links to related hypotheses or decisions

## Null results matter

Record failed tests, null results, and negative evidence when they change confidence or prevent repeated wasted work.

## Promotion path

1. Capture idea in `HYPOTHESIS_LAB.md`.
2. Test or investigate in `RESEARCH_LOG.md`.
3. If the result changes direction, record a decision in `DECISION_LOG.md`.
4. If the conclusion becomes current truth, update `CURRENT_STATE.md`.
5. If human priorities change, update `HUMAN_BRIEF.md`.
6. Add a recovery checkpoint.

## Confidence language

Use practical confidence labels:

- `high`: multiple checks or strong evidence; low risk of reversal
- `medium`: plausible and supported, but limited by sample, method, or assumptions
- `low`: early signal, weak evidence, or high uncertainty

Never hide limitations to make the log look cleaner.
